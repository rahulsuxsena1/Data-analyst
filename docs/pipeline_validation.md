## Where the sentiment model's errors come from

- Test-set errors: 263 of 2300 reviews.
- Planted rating 'misclicks' (rating contradicts the text): 23% of errors vs 3% of all test reviews.
- Planted sarcasm: 0% of errors vs 1% of all test reviews.
- Misclicks are heavily over-represented among errors: a large part of the 'error' is in the weak labels, not the model.

## Sarcasm: an honest caveat

- VADER scores **69%** of the 136 sarcastic reviews as positive. A lexicon sees 'fantastic' and 'love' and cannot see irony.
- TF-IDF + logistic regression classifies **97%** of sarcastic test reviews correctly. **Do not quote this as a sarcasm result.** The generator reuses six sarcastic sentences, so the model memorised them. Real sarcasm is not templated; expect this to fall sharply on real data.

## What this proves, and what it does not

- It proves the **method** finds the kinds of problems it claims to find, at roughly the strength they were planted.
- It does **not** prove anything about a real car market. The data is synthetic.
- With real data, replace this file with a hand-labelled sample of ~200 reviews and report the same precision / recall table.

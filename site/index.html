Automotive Customer Sentiment & Business Intelligence

The most common complaint isn't the one worth fixing.

A car company gets thousands of reviews. I built a pipeline that cleans them, finds the complaint themes, measures how much each one actually costs the star rating, and raises an alert when a new problem appears, then shows it all in a Power BI dashboard.

    06 · How I know it works

    The analysis ran blind, then was checked against the planted answers

    Real review data has no answer key, so you can never be sure a method missed something. The generator planted known problems and saved the answers separately. No analysis script was allowed to read them. At the end, one script compared the results with the answers.

    Theme tagger vs the true themes

    High precision; recall limited by indirect phrasing such as "towed twice in six months"

    Sentiment models, held-out test set

    Against rating-based (weak) labels. Templated synthetic text flatters text models

      What this project does not claim

      • Synthetic data. It proves the method, not the findings.
      • Weak labels. Star ratings are an imperfect stand-in for sentiment.
      • Keywords miss things. Indirect phrasing and sarcasm; VADER reads 69% of sarcastic reviews as positive.
      • Association, not causation. The regression shows what goes with low ratings, not what causes them.
      • No sales data. Scenarios estimate rating changes only.
